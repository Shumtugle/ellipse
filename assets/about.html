THE WEATHER AND THE AIR

Weather, air quality, pollen and the search for places come from Open-Meteo, under the licence CC BY 4.0.
The geomagnetic index, for the aurora, comes from the Space Weather Prediction Center of NOAA, in the public domain.

SYMBOLS

The symbols an icon may wear are Material Symbols, under the Apache License 2.0.

WHAT LEAVES THE PHONE

Only what the weather needs: the place chosen, or where the phone roughly is, sent to the weather's source to ask for its forecast. Nothing else is sent anywhere: no counts, no statistics, no advertising. Backups go only where they are saved.
