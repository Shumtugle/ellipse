# Ellipse

**Page & download → https://shumtugle.github.io/ellipse/**

A home screen for Android, made one small release at a time: icons cut from materials, ten clock faces, a wallpaper factory, presets and a random look, folders on the screens and in the list of every app, a night clock, a page of the phone's state, and a status strip of its own.

Every part of this project — screens, materials, logic, packaging — was generated by AI (Anthropic Claude Opus 5.5) through plain conversation, and tried on a phone after each release. No Android Studio, no Gradle, no libraries: plain Java against `android.jar`, built by one short script.

**Package:** `io.github.shumtugle.ellipse` · **Version:** 2.18.0 · **Android** 8.0+ · **Languages:** English, Russian, Finnish

## On the screens
- **Icons** — outlines from circle to a wide paper tile; rims of metal, gold, wood, steel, black, glass, the accent, silk (pink, nude, gold, silver) and mesh (dark, light); embossing inside the plate; symbols in round windows; icon packs; a picture, a symbol or a mark of the dice for any app
- **Grid** — any number of screens, a home one among them, a dock or none, margins or everything to the edges, icons that fill their places whole
- **Ten clock faces** — the first, a plate, a glass card, bubbles (moved and sized by hand), eclipse, horizon, reservoir, flip cards, a monogram, three stones; each shows the time, the weather and the charge, and the headphones' charge while they are near
- **Wallpaper** — the phone's own chooser (live wallpapers too) and its framing of a picture; a picture kept by Ellipse; or the factory: grounds in layers (a light, a texture, an ornament), dice for new ones, a new ground while the phone sleeps
- **Presets** — Material 3, Random, Steel, Meno, Bubbles, Paper, Walnut, Gold, Noir; tried on before they are taken; lucky random looks remembered
- **Folders** — on the screens (faces of four, nine, a ring, a stack; widgets inside) and in the list of every app
- **Widgets** — framed in the icons' materials, stacked in one place, set into folders
- **Night clock** — the clock across the whole screen on its side, darker, into full black when untouched
- **Own status strip** — where the hidden status bar stood: signal, Wi-Fi, unread, date, charge; dark on a light wallpaper
- **Lying down** — every screen seen whole, sideways

## Pages
- **Weather** — now, the next two hours in quarters, hours, days, air, pollen, aurora ([Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), NOAA)
- **State** — charge, free memory and storage as rings; battery health and cycles; security and system updates; the day as the home screen counted it
- **Copies** — one file with every screen, setting and look, and a small picture of the home screen; open a copy file anywhere and it shows itself before it comes back; backups of some other launchers come in too

## Touch
- **Time → the clock · Date → the calendar · Weather → the weather page · Charge → the phone's state · Headphones → Bluetooth**
- **Status strip:** swipe down on the left for notifications, on the right for quick settings

## Permissions
- **Internet** — the weather, the air, the place search; nothing else leaves the phone
- **Location (coarse)** — weather where you are; optional
- **Set wallpaper** — the factory's grounds and your own picture
- **Bluetooth** — the headphones' charge
- **Expand status bar** — gestures and the strip lower the shade
- **Uninstall request** — Uninstall in an icon's menu
- Asked only when turned on: **notification access** (dots, unread, player), **Do Not Disturb access** (the night clock's quiet), **all files access** (a wallpaper set elsewhere, kept in a copy), **device admin / accessibility** (locking the screen by a gesture)

## Structure
```
AndroidManifest.xml
build.sh                 the whole build: aapt, javac, dx, zipalign, apksigner
bundle.sh                an app bundle, for a store that wants one
src/io/github/shumtugle/ellipse/    the home screen, in plain Java
res/                     icons, pictures of materials, a few strings
assets/                  the help and about pages, weather and place data
assets/lang/             language modules: ru.txt, fi.txt, and template.txt to write another
tools/words.py           gathers every phrase of the interface into the template
art/icons.py             draws the launcher's icon
```

## Building
```bash
export SDK=/path/to/android-33.jar
export KEYSTORE=/path/to/your.keystore KSPASS=your-password
sh build.sh ellipse-2.18.0
# -> out/ellipse-2.18.0.apk
```
Needs `aapt`, `javac` (8+), `dalvik-exchange` (the dx tool), `zipalign` and `apksigner` on the path.
